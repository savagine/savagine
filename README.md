## Hey, I’m Bruno, a.k.a. sqvage.

I'm from New York, and I'm the founder and CEO of **DRAG**.

I've never been good at stopping at the basics. I'm always looking for something new to try, and when a topic really grabs me, I keep digging until I understand what's going on underneath. A lot of what I build starts the same way: I see something, I think it could be done better, and I go do it. Sometimes the only reason is pure ambition, and that's reason enough for me.

That mindset is what I bring to DRAG. I care about the details people never notice, because those details decide whether something merely works or is really well made.

I don't stick to one role. At DRAG, I write the code, run the business, and talk to the customers, so I get to see everything from the first idea to the last support ticket. Being that close to all of it teaches me things that writing code alone never would.

## How I work

- **I learn from the inside.** I'd rather understand how something works than just know how to use it.
- **"It works" is only the starting point.** After that come the questions that matter: can it be faster, safer, or simpler?
- **I plan for things going wrong.** Good software shows its quality when conditions are bad, not when everything goes right.
- **I finish what I start.** Tests, security, and the last small details are part of the job, not extras.
- **I keep experimenting.** New languages and new problems are how I keep growing, and they're also where the fun is.
